Our Beginning

Born from a deep appreciation for authentic culinary traditions, ALMAS is a leading Hyderabadi restaurant on Ellesmere Road. We bring with us a commitment to preserving heritage recipes while presenting them with a modern, elevated touch.

Every element of our offering is thoughtfully curated to deliver not just a meal, but a memorable experience rooted in culture and craftsmanship. Inspired by the royal kitchens of Hyderabad, we present an experience that celebrates tradition, flavor, and sophistication in every detail.

Our Vision

To be a distinguished destination where the timeless depth of Indian heritage and the freshness of Lebanese cuisine come together to create an unforgettable culinary journey.

Our Mission

To honor the legacy of Indian cuisine through authentic Hyderabadi slow-cooked traditions, enriched with the vibrant nuances of Lebanese flavors, crafting a refined and memorable dining experience.
